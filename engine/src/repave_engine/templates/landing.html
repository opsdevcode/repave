What the platform does

Platform owns the roads. Builders consume them. When a standard moves, you re-render from the blueprint instead of patching every repo by hand.

  • Golden paths

    Terraform modules, Helm charts, Ansible, policy, and app services from the same catalog. Short form, versioned pins, preview before apply.

  • Gates that block

    Policy and quality run on every apply. Failures stop the write. Regulated families cannot turn that off without a waiver.

  • Lineage you can upgrade

    Each repo records its blueprint and standard pins. Plan an upgrade, open a PR, and merge the mechanical pin bumps that are allowed.

  • Hosted catalog

    Ownership, scaffolding, and catalog-info.yaml live in Backstage. The engine stays CLI and /api/v2.

How it works

  1. Pick a path Choose a golden path from the catalog — module, service, policy, or bundle.
  2. Configure Answer a short form. Pins and scope stay versioned with the blueprint.
  3. Plan repave renders a deterministic artifact. You preview the plan before apply.
  4. Gates Policy and quality gates run on every apply. Failures block the write.
  5. Publish Created repos land in the catalog with lineage you can upgrade later.
